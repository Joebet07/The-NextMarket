A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Frequently asked questions

How does shipping work?

Standard shipping is ₱150 per order, with estimated delivery in 3–5 business days within Metro Manila and 5–7 business days nationwide.

How do I compare product prices?

Open any product page or visit the Market Trends page to view recent sale prices, lowest ask, and highest bid figures.

What do the condition grades mean?

Like New means minimal signs of use. Excellent means light wear. Good means visible wear but fully functional. Fair means heavier cosmetic wear.

Can I edit or remove a listing?

Yes. Open the Sell page, scroll down to My listings, and click Edit or Delete.
